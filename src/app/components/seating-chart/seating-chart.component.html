📋 Data Entry Guide

Supported date formats when entering data in the Google Sheet:

Single Date Column G

Select a date cell directly in Google Sheets — stored as a date type automatically.

July 1 2026

Date Range Column H

Two full dates separated by – (space‑hyphen‑space), same or different months.

June 2 2026 - June 3 2026

Same-month Multi-date Column H

Month & year prefix, then comma-separated dates or sub-ranges — all within the same month.

June 2026 - 9, 10, 16-18, 23-24, 30
  • 9, 10, 30 — individual dates
  • 16-18 or 16 - 18 — range (16, 17, 18)
  • 23-24 — range (23, 24)

⚠️ Leave Column G empty when using Column H (Date Range), and vice versa.
